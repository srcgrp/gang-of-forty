# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-25

### Added
- Persian RTL grocery shop with product categories, offer rows, product details, and sorting.
- Cart state that is saved between page visits, plus stock and offer information from the API.
- Docker setup, CI checks, and Vercel deployment configuration.

### Changed
- Added shared light and dark themes and improved the mobile layout.
- Added client-side routes for product lists and product details.

### Fixed
- Improved the product loading retry message and discounted-price layout.
